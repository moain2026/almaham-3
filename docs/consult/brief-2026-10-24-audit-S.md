## System (unchanged from brief #1)
Offline Android reader for electricity-meter LCDs (Yemen). Two tiny CenterNet-style detectors (DigitNet, 0.21M params, stride 4, heat+wh+offset), ONNX Runtime on phone:
- LOCATOR: 256x256 letterboxed meter crop (user draws a rectangle around the whole meter) → 1 class LINE = union of the INTEGER digit boxes of the kWh line.
- READER: the LINE box padded (PAD_X 0.18, PAD_Y 0.45) → 64x448 letterbox → 11 classes (digits 0-9 + dot). `assemble.py` groups digits, cuts integer part (dot between digits | height drop <0.65 | if ≥7 digits in a regular lattice cut at 6 | large gap), statuses OK/EMPTY/MISSING_DIGIT/TOO_MANY_DIGITS, `confident` iff all digits score ≥0.45 and runner-up <0.5×best. - Product rule: integer kWh only; "0.0 error" = a confidently-returned reading must never be wrong; leaving empty is fine. Speed is not a constraint (full pipeline ≈ 93 ms on x86 server).
- Training: `erp_finetune.py`: AdamW 2e-4 cosine, 3000 steps, batch 32 (reader) / 24 (locator), every batch 50% real / 50% synthetic (line_s1 4000 synthetic 7-seg lines; scene_s1 3000 synthetic meters), focal + 0.1·L1(wh) + L1(off), AUG v2 (one affine: rot ±8°, shear 0.15, scale 0.8–1.2, shift 0.06; photometric: brightness/contrast/gamma/HSV/gray, blur, noise, JPEG 35–95, glare 0.2, channel-perm, digit-perm 0.2).
